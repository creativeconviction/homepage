We’re Libbie and Rayouf.

Creative Conviction is a series of conversations with people exceptional at their craft. We talk to founders, developers, designers, studio owners and artists about how they think, where they find inspiration, and how they make decisions.

Technology is changing how everything gets made. Our mission is to understand what that means for creativity, and help more builders find their own creative path by learning from those who have forged theirs.

We’re just getting started, with more interviews, media formats, and mechanisms of bringing this community together. Tell us who or what to feature next!
